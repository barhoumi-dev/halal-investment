2.

Business Overview

IREN (formerly Iris Energy) is a vertically integrated data-center operator headquartered in Sydney, with its main operating footprint in Childress, Texas, plus sites in British Columbia and a newly announced 800MW campus in Bundey, South Australia. The company owns its land, grid connections, electrical infrastructure and buildings, and monetizes that power capacity through two businesses: Bitcoin mining (one of the largest listed self-mining fleets) and an AI cloud / GPU colocation business that has become the growth engine.

The pivotal development is the ~$9.7B multi-year AI cloud contract with Microsoft announced in late 2025, which IREN is servicing with NVIDIA Blackwell-generation GPUs. To fund delivery, IREN closed a $3.65B investment-grade GPU financing facility on 2026-06-01 and signed a ~$1.6B purchase agreement with Dell for air-cooled Blackwell systems (2026-05-26). Management targets ~$4.4B of annualized run-rate revenue (ARR) from the Childress Blackwell deployment alone.

Revenue today is still majority Bitcoin mining, transitioning rapidly toward contracted AI cloud revenue. The moat is cheap contracted renewable-heavy power, owned grid interconnects, and speed of energization — scarce inputs in the post-2025 AI buildout. Co-founders Daniel and William Roberts run the company; insider ownership is meaningful for a company that has diluted heavily (shares out +52% YoY).

Catalysts & Risks

Catalysts

  • FY Q4 / FY2026 results (~Aug 2026) — first quarter with material Blackwell revenue contribution; watch AI Cloud ARR run-rate vs the $4.4B Childress target.
  • Childress Blackwell energization milestones through 2H 2026 — Dell deliveries against the $1.6B order convert directly into contracted revenue.
  • A second hyperscaler contract — Bernstein frames a $5.5B two-part opportunity; any signing re-rates the stock.
  • South Australia 800MW campus — financing/anchor-tenant announcements would validate the multi-GW pipeline.
  • Bitcoin price recovery — mining still carries near-term revenue; BTC weakness has been dragging the whole complex (CNBC, 2026-06-03).

Risks

  • Customer concentration: Microsoft is the anchor of the AI pivot — any delivery dispute, delay or renegotiation hits ~$9.7B of contracted backlog.
  • Financing treadmill: -$2.3B TTM FCF funded by $3.97B debt + 52% YoY share dilution; if capital markets close or rates spike, the growth model stalls.
  • BTC drawdown: mining revenue and sentiment beta (4.23) mean a crypto bear market can halve the stock irrespective of AI execution — June's BTC slide already took IREN down ~25% from highs.
  • GPU depreciation risk: $1.6B+ of Blackwell hardware depreciating over ~5 years; if AI cloud pricing compresses before payback, unit economics break.
  • Grid/permitting delays at Sweetwater/South Australia push ARR conversion to the right while interest expense accrues now.
  • Halal-mandate risk (specific to this portfolio): the name now fails every Shariah lens — see Section 3.

In eight quarters IREN went from debt-free to ~$4B of debt while nearly doubling the share count. That is a conscious strategy — sell equity when the stock is expensive, borrow against contracted GPU revenue when lenders will underwrite it ('investment-grade GPU financing'). It has worked because the stock 8x'd in a year (+760% market cap YoY). The fragility: every leg of new growth (South Australia, a second hyperscaler) requires fresh capital, so the equity story depends on markets staying open. Short interest in the neocloud complex is elevated (Invezz, 2026-05-21) — traders are explicitly betting the funding cycle breaks.

10.

Summary & Thesis

Bull case: IREN owns the scarcest assets in AI infrastructure — energized power, land and grid connections — and has converted them into a ~$9.7B Microsoft contract with a credible path to $4.4B ARR from Childress alone, with the Australian land bank as a free option. At ~6.8x EV/FY27E revenue, the market is not paying a premium for the option value; consensus targets ($81 avg, $126 high) imply 50–130% upside if execution continues.

Bear case: the model consumes capital faster than it generates it (-$2.3B TTM FCF), GAAP losses are widening into the ramp, the share count compounds at 50%+, and the whole complex trades at 4.2 beta to BTC and AI sentiment. A single stumble — Microsoft delivery slip, BTC bear market, credit window closing — forces dilution at depressed prices. Goldman's $50 Hold and two Sell ratings are not fringe views.

For this portfolio specifically: IREN is now NOT Shariah-compliant on all four lenses (Zoya, Musaffa, AAOIFI, PIF). Under a halal mandate the investment verdict is overridden — the position warrants review for exit with purification of ~$0.13/share of attributable interest income, regardless of the attractive commercial thesis.

What to watch next

  • FY2026 results (~Aug 2026): AI Cloud ARR run-rate and the FY27 revenue guide vs $3.08B consensus.
  • Monthly operational updates: Blackwell racks energized at Childress vs plan.
  • Quarterly 10-Q interest income/expense lines — the Shariah screen would only heal if the cash pile shrinks into CapEx and debt is restructured; re-run the PIF screen each quarter.
