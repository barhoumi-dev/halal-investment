2.

Business Overview

TSMC manufactures the chips everyone else designs — over 90% of the world's leading-edge (≤5nm) logic capacity, including every NVIDIA data-center GPU, every Apple silicon die, and the custom accelerators of Google, Amazon and Microsoft. The foundry model means TSMC monetizes the entire AI buildout regardless of which chip designer wins. Q1 2026 gross margin hit a record 66.3% on N3/N2 pricing power.

The numbers are historic: FY2025 revenue +31.6% (TWD 3.81T) and FY2026 consensus calls for +35.6% more — for a company this size, unprecedented. The CEO's June message was that AI demand will outstrip supply for years; capacity is so tight that The Information reports Google and NVIDIA are exploring Intel as a backup — a headline that flatters TSMC's position even as it seeds the long-term second-source narrative.

Moat: process leadership (N2 ramping, A16 next), unmatched yield learning, advanced packaging (CoWoS — the AI bottleneck), and an ecosystem of 500+ customers who co-optimize with TSMC for years. Risks are mostly exogenous: Taiwan-strait geopolitics, US export-control tightening (bipartisan Senate letter this week urging restrictions on supplying Chinese firms' overseas units), and the cost drag of US/Japan/Germany fab diversification.

3c. PIF 4-step screen

Step 1 — Core business. Semiconductor fabrication is halal. Checklist: all twelve excluded industries — N. (Chips end up in everything, including defense systems, but the screen tests TSMC's own business: contract wafer fabrication for commercial fabless customers.)

Step 2 — Israel presence / human rights. TSMC's fabs are Taiwan, US (Arizona, Washington), Japan, Germany and China (Nanjing/Shanghai, mature nodes). No Israeli operations or R&D. Customer-side, Israeli fabless designers use TSMC like everyone else — customer activity, not company footprint. No HRW/Amnesty/BDS flags on TSMC. Step 2: PASS.

Catalysts & Risks

Catalysts

  • Q2 2026 earnings 2026-07-16 — watch FY26 revenue guide (consensus +35.6%) and any capex raise; monthly revenue prints (10th of each month) are the real-time tell.
  • N2 ramp economics — second-half 2026 volume production with reported strong pricing; first N2 margin data point re-rates FY27 estimates.
  • CoWoS/advanced packaging expansion — every increment is sold out; capacity adds convert directly to revenue.
  • AI sovereign/hyperscaler prepayments — customers paying deposits to reserve capacity (the ASML-ization of the foundry model).
  • Dividend acceleration — TWD 7.0/q (+40% YoY) signals a payout step-change as FCF inflects to TWD 1.47T.

Risks

  • Taiwan-strait geopolitics — the permanent tail risk; any blockade/conflict scenario is uninsurable at the position level and explains the structural multiple discount.
  • US export-control expansion (this week's Senate letter) — compliance burden plus potential loss of China-linked revenue at the margin.
  • AI capex digestion 2027 — if hyperscaler orders pause, the +35% comp becomes a cliff; semis short interest at records (CNBC, Jun 9) shows the positioning.
  • Overseas fab margin dilution — Arizona/Dresden/Kumamoto cost structures shave 2–3 GM points as they scale.
  • Intel second-sourcing — Google/NVIDIA backup exploration is today a capacity story; by 2028 it could be a pricing story.
  • Shariah-mandate: PIF Step 3 borderline-fails on the stale window (Section 3) — monitor quarterly; do not add until a clean window prints if applying PIF strictly.

TSMC trades at roughly half the PEG of every other critical-path AI name. That gap is the market's price for Taiwan risk — roughly $500–700B of suppressed market value. The investment question is whether you are being paid enough to hold the risk; at 0.46 PEG with the dividend growing 30%+ annually, the answer for a sized position (10 ADRs here) has historically been yes. The position sizing itself is the hedge: geography risk argues against making TSM a portfolio-dominant weight, not against owning it.

10.

Summary & Thesis

Bull case: the indispensable company of the AI era growing revenue 35% at record 66% gross margins, with a sold-out advanced-packaging franchise, a fortress balance sheet funding $50B+ capex from cash flow, and a valuation (22x forward, 0.46 PEG) that still embeds a deep geopolitical discount. Street consensus sees +9% to the median target with zero sell ratings.

Bear case: the cycle and the strait. A 2027 hyperscaler digestion year would compress both the E and the P/E simultaneously (the bear target $354 is only a 17x multiple on current-year earnings); export-control expansion chips at China-linked revenue; overseas fabs dilute the margin peak; and the tail risk that defines the discount cannot be diversified away within the position.

Shariah status: borderline — AAOIFI/Zoya/Musaffa all pass; the strict PIF four-quarter window fails on Q2–Q3 2025 interest income (2.6–2.7% vs 2.5%) with a clearly improving trend as revenue outgrows the cash yield. Treatment consistent with this portfolio's DT precedent: hold, purify ~$0.66/ADR/year, re-run the screen at Q2 2026 results. As an investment, the highest-conviction core AI holding in the portfolio at the most reasonable price.
